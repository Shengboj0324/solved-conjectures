## Partial results on open problems

- **Wild-number conjecture for complete graphs.** For every $n\ge155$ and every minimum proper edge colouring of $K_n$, the wild number equals $\lfloor4\lceil n/2\rceil/3\rfloor$. The range $13\le n\le154$ remains open. [Proofs and exact checks](results/wild-number-complete-graphs/).
- [Seven upper-half circular chromatic index certificates](results/circular-index-upper-half-remaining-deltas/). Tomdif’s examples at degrees $9,11,13,15,17,19,23$, together with the earlier notes, cover every maximum degree $\Delta\ge8$. [Lean proof for every degree](results/circular-index-upper-half-remaining-deltas/FORMALIZATION.md).
- [Upper-half circular chromatic index for degrees congruent to 1 or 2 modulo 4](results/circular-index-upper-half-delta-2-mod-4/). Constructions for degrees $\Delta=4g+1$ and $4g+2$ with $g\ge6$, plus finite certificates for degrees $10,14,18,22$. [Lean proof for every parameter](results/circular-index-upper-half-delta-2-mod-4/FORMALIZATION.md). [Lean certificates for the 17 stored nonregular graphs](results/circular-index-upper-half-circulants/FORMALIZATION.md).
- [Upper-half circular chromatic index for degrees congruent to 3 modulo 4](results/circular-index-upper-half-delta-3-mod-4/). Examples for every $\Delta\equiv3\pmod4$ with $\Delta\ge27$. Together with [tomdif’s seven certificates](results/circular-index-upper-half-remaining-deltas/), the combined results cover every maximum degree $\Delta\ge8$. [Lean proofs of Theorem A, Proposition B and Corollary C](results/circular-index-upper-half-delta-3-mod-4/FORMALIZATION.md).
- **Shifted Lonely Runner.** Lean verifies the 40 exact counterexamples to Zhang’s Question 8.5 for $18\le n\le57$. The range $58\le n\le94$ remains open. [Counterexamples and verification](results/shifted-lonely-runner-counterexamples/).
- **Steinke–Zakynthinou’s agnostic VC learning conjecture.** Lean proves the companion paper’s distribution-free Conjecture 4.18 for every positive sample size. For $`n\ge d`$, the same proper learner attains fixed-pairing information at most $`19{,}712d`$ nats and expected empirical excess at most $`583\sqrt{d/n}`$ on every sample. For the iid version, a written disproof uses standard Borel names and jointly universally measurable evaluation. Other Lean-verified statements give positive and negative results under distinct measurability hypotheses. [Manuscript and Lean verification](results/steinke-zakynthinou-agnostic-vc/) · [Written iid disproof](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/research/research/steinke-zakynthinou-agnostic-vc/universally-measurable-evaluation-obstruction.md)
- **Erdős essential-component conjecture.** Essentiality of the numbers $2^m3^n$ is equivalent to uniform one-sided expansion of half-density sets modulo $2^k$. Expansion is at least of order $(\log k)^{3/2}/k$, and small boundaries project to shorter moduli under the full projected shift set. [Research note](docs/erdos-essential-component-research.md) · [Issue #73](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/73).
- **Erdős–Sauer conjecture.** For every fixed uniformity, the maximum hypergraph clique-and-singleton decomposition number agrees asymptotically with the corresponding Turán number. The exact bound holds with at most two cliques through each edge. [Research note](docs/erdos-sauer-research.md) · [Issue #72](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/72).
- **Bollobás–Erdős–Tuza conjecture.** Graphs with all maximal independent sets of linear size can require transversals of order $\sqrt{n\log n}$. Bounded modular width gives a constant transversal bound. [Research note](docs/bollobas-erdos-tuza-research.md) · [Issue #71](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/71).
- **Erdős–Oler conjecture.** The packing bound holds on unit-separated lattices, and triangular arrays with one site removed have a separation radius independent of their order. In a counterexample, thin Delaunay faces must occur near at least two sides of the containing triangle. [Packing and rigidity](docs/erdos-oler-research.md) · [Boundary faces](docs/erdos-oler-boundary-faces.md) · [Issue #62](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/62).
- **Erdős–Granville–Pomerance–Spiro conjecture.** The preimage of every natural density-zero set under the sum-of-proper-divisors map has density zero within a fixed input family of natural density about 53.46%. The arguments use three second-moment estimates for different prime-factor ranges. [Scope and proofs](results/erdos-granville-pomerance-spiro/) · [Finite checks](results/erdos-granville-pomerance-spiro/verify.py).
- **Frankl’s conjecture (partial).** Generators supported in four consecutive positions of a common cyclic order guarantee an abundant point. For uniform generators with every point in three generators and incidence girth at least 16, each point occurs in at least 51.8208% of the unions. [Local proof](results/frankl-generator-families/PROOF.md) · [Frequency proof](results/frankl-generator-families/HIGH-GIRTH.md) · [Replay](results/frankl-generator-families/) Both all-point frequency bounds are fully Lean-verified; the cyclic span-four formalization remains incomplete. [Checked scope](docs/lean-status.md#complete-frankl-high-girth-frequency-theorem).
- **Lonely Runner (partial).** Two arbitrary positive integer speeds can be added to any Goddyn–Wong simultaneous acceleration. The proof gives a prescribed interval for the common witness and an obstruction to the corresponding three-speed assertion. [Proof](results/lonely-runner-two-insertions/PROOF.md) · [Replay](results/lonely-runner-two-insertions/) The complete insertion note is Lean-verified, including the unbounded speeds, acceleration criterion, common-dilation value bound, and literal three-insertion obstruction. [Checked scope](docs/lean-status.md#complete-unrestricted-two-speed-insertion-theorem).
- **Erdős–Gyárfás (partial).** Every abelian cover of $K_4$ with one edge subdivided has a cycle of length 4, 8, 16 or 32. A 19-sheeted cyclic example attains the upper bound. The universal voltage-cover theorem, literal 95-vertex sharpness example, and normalization of all seven voltages are Lean-verified. [Lean scope](docs/lean-status.md#complete-sharp-95-vertex-example-and-arbitrary-voltage-normalization). [Proof](results/erdos-gyarfas-abelian-covers/PROOF.md) · [Replay](results/erdos-gyarfas-abelian-covers/)
- **Hadamard existence conjecture, order 2396 (open).** The signed automorphism group of any candidate matrix has order coprime to 479. The fixed-border common multiplier group is cyclic of order dividing four, with at least 151 sign orbits. Lean verifies these statements, both norm-obstruction proofs, the complete conditional [fixed-border construction](results/hadamard-2396/BORDER.md) from its original four sign rows, all fourteen size-149 baseline patterns, and the length-598 order-eleven obstruction. Lean also constructs the actual Hadamard matrix from any Legendre pair and proves the invariant quotient transport from the original centered rows. The order and orbit theorems for odd automorphism subgroups on groups of order nine are also checked. For length-1197 Legendre pairs, Lean proves that the common-multiplier order divides 27; the stated sharper bound of nine and the exhaustive quotient/no-lift certificates remain pending. No order-2396 matrix existence is claimed. [Checked scope and receipts](docs/lean-status.md#complete-conditional-fixed-border-construction-and-fourteen-patterns) · [Automorphism proof](results/hadamard-2396/AUTOMORPHISMS.md) · [Sequence proof](results/hadamard-2396/PROOF.md) · [Reproduce](results/hadamard-2396/).

## Precise scope

The gamma-quotient result proves the full all-order assertion in Karp–Vishnyakova–Zhang's Section 3, item 11 for all positive gamma arguments and every $c-d>0$. The parameter-dependent signs and integer finite-rank cases belong to this single theorem. The subsidiary order-three sign correction is not counted separately.

The Sagan–Swanson result proves the full four-family asymptotic-normality Conjecture 7.8, with no rate restriction on convergence of $k/n$ to a fixed value in $(0,1)$. The proof controls the conditioning event and derives the actual conditional means and variances. It does not claim the separate coefficient log-concavity, parity log-concavity, bottom-heaviness, or boundary-regime questions.

The Bernardi–Morales result proves the complete delta-process tree-probability conjecture and its stronger event-determinant conjecture: published numbers 27–28, preprint numbers 21–22. The tree proof allows arbitrary fixed color maps, including nonsurjections; the event proof uses a fixed surjection, as in the source. Both statements are recorded under one named investigation. The two exact verifiers check finite instances independently of the general determinant arguments.

Gilmore's matching identity concerns his product-defined matrix and proves the complete published Conjecture 6.16 (arXiv Conjecture 6.11). The neighboring shifted reverse Bessel Hankel-total-positivity conjecture remains open.

Chow's weak consecutive-interlacing assertion follows immediately from established stronger refinement results. The note credits those results and does not claim a new compatibility theorem. The current literature audit found no earlier explicit resolution of the consecutive assertion; that is a bounded search, not proof of priority.

The L-solitaire counterexample relies on the original paper's Definitions 2--4 allowing *positive*, not necessarily diverging, horizontal scaling factors. It does **not** refute the natural corrected assertion for diverging scales. The note supplies a proof of that corrected assertion for all admissible layer sets.

The necklace result refutes **only** Pham's exact-degree guess. It does **not** refute the distinct Harris--Nguyen (2025) conjecture that a necklace and its reverse-complement dual admit a common denominator of degree equal to their period. The included dual calculation is consistent with that conjecture.

The Li example is neither a spider nor a caterpillar and refutes the **non-spider** sign prediction. Earlier spider constructions already refute the unrestricted coefficient assertion; likewise, earlier literature contains trees with two degree-4 vertices. The newly reported result is explicitly limited to the non-spider class, and novelty is provisional pending independent review (see the [novelty audit](results/li-coefficient-sign/NOVELTY.md)). This example does **not** refute Dahlberg--She--van Willigenburg's general non-`e`-positivity conjecture.

For each runner count from 18 through 57, Lean proves that the recorded shifts and distinct positive integer speeds have the claimed global maximum over real time. Each maximum is strictly less than $1/(n+1)$. For $41\le n\le57$ the speed sets differ from $1,\ldots,n$. Zhang’s question stays open for $58\le n\le94$, as does the classification of minimizing configurations. [Lean proof and receipt](results/shifted-lonely-runner-counterexamples/FORMALIZATION.md).

The [wild-number result](results/wild-number-complete-graphs/) proves Dawkins–Nappo–Tong–Zimbizi's Conjecture 6.1 only for `n >= 155`; with the source's `n <= 12`, every `n` with `13 <= n <= 154` remains open. The method, a local-lemma-type criterion for a uniformly random triple partition, provably fails for odd `141 <= n <= 153` in the form used, so closing the gap needs a different argument. The random searches over sample factorizations in the open range are evidence, not proof. The continuation material, including the reduction of `n = 13, 14` to a finite statement and the partial cube-and-conquer run, settles no new value. Both referee passes were made by model instances; no Lean formalization is included.

These are mathematical claims with explicit proofs and computer checks, **not peer-reviewed publications**. A literature search cannot establish that a counterexample or stronger theorem is the first discovery. Please cite the primary publications when discussing the original conjectures.
