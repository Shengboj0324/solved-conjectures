# Statement, scope, and prior-resolution search

- Per Alexandersson posted the graphical dist conjecture on 23 November 2026. It asks about *every vertex order*. The source reports an exhaustive check on labeled graphs through seven vertices, and a theorem for chordal graphs in a reverse perfect elimination order. Neither restricts the order in the conjecture. The source also asks a separate question about all orientations, for which our conditional triangle rule does not apply. [Original issue](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2641)
- The cross-referenced result concerns distinct-value polynomials for independent entries ranging uniformly from zero to specified bounds. It applies to reverse perfect elimination orders. In the counterexamples the earlier-neighbor counts at the distinguished vertices depend jointly on the direction of a shared edge. [Companion result](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2640)
- Alexandersson and Leite study distributions of the number of **sinks**, including positive results for chordal claw-free graphs and counterexamples in line graphs. A sink compares all incident edges. Here the statistic counts the different numbers of edges coming in from earlier vertices. [Alexandersson–Leite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41502)
- On 26 November 2026, searches for the exact conjecture name, graphical distinct-value polynomials, graph-restricted inversion tables, earlier indegrees of acyclic orientations, and disproofs under the author’s name returned the source issue and related work on different orientation statistics. GitHub issue and timeline searches located the independent-entry theorem above. We found no prior resolution of this exact conjecture. [Source discussion](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2641) · [Author’s graph-polynomial catalogue](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedGraphs.htm)
