# Source and priority

Per Alexandersson states the conjecture for full-support tables of arbitrary positive integral margins. Both margin vectors must contain a nonunit entry. The primary research issue opened November 22, 2026. Its November 23 status audit records an earlier counterexample to a separate extension permitting forbidden cells. There a forced final row leaves a Birkhoff polytope. The November 24 update reports computations on one-parameter families and keeps the full-support question open. The present family allows every cell. [Author’s conjecture](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm) · [Primary research issue](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2627) · [Interval-support counterexample](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2627#issuecomment-7110080597) · [Prior computational update](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2627#issuecomment-7144671629)

The related root-polytope literature identifies the Ehrhart numerator of the edge polytope of a connected bipartite graph with its interior polynomial. The present polytope projects by its first two rows onto the convex hull of the lattice points $(e_i,e_j)$ with all $i,j$ allowed except $i=j$ at a unit column. In graph terms this is $K_{m+1,m+1}$ with a matching of size $m$ deleted. We checked this alias as well as the original transportation terminology. Root nonreality for other graph polytopes and unit-margin examples precedes this question. [Kálmán–Postnikov](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.04449) · [Ohsugi–Tsuchiya](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.12258)


Xiaxia Guan and Xian’an Jin had already computed the numerator formula in the graph formulation. Their Theorem 53 gives the interior polynomial of a complete bipartite graph with any matching deleted, including the exact polynomials used here. The proof above gives the specialization directly from the transportation margins. Guan and Jin use hypertree activities and determine coefficients. Their paper contains no assertion about nonreal zeros of these polynomials. The additional steps here are the rational violation of real-rootedness and the unbounded family of nonreal roots, applied to Alexandersson’s full-support conjecture. [Guan–Jin, Theorem 53](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12531)

On November 26, 2026, we searched both repository catalogues, issues, the proposer’s issues and pull requests, arXiv, web-indexed papers, and OpenAlex under transportation, deleted-matching, crown-graph, edge-polytope, and interior-polynomial terminology. OpenAlex listed three works citing Guan and Jin. Their topics are the polymatroid Tutte polynomial, pseudo-Gorenstein edge rings, and a recursion for interior polynomials. We found no prior disproof for the full-support statement or prior nonreal-root theorem for this specific family in that search. Independent mathematical review covered the full-support table count, the exact Laguerre and Sturm calculations at $m=12$, and the Legendre estimates, endpoint cases and multiplicity counts for the asymptotic theorem. The reviewer independently computed 2, 2, 4 and 6 nonreal roots for $m=13,20,32,48$, respectively.
