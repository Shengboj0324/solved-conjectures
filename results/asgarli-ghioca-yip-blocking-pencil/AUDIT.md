# Source, priority and proof checks

## The question in the primary source

In §5 of *Blocking sets from a union of plane curves*, Shamil Asgarli, Dragos Ghioca and Chi Hoi Yip define the pencil by $YZ^{d-1}=X^d-\alpha Z^d$. Their Theorem 5.1 gives the logarithmic upper bound. Remark 5.3 gives a logarithmic lower bound when $\gcd(d,q-1)>1$. The paragraph following Question 5.4 explicitly asks for the asymptotic behavior in the permutation regime. The present theorem determines that order for every fixed exponent and all prime powers. The exact minimum for a general exponent and a sharp leading constant are separate questions. The original Erdős bounded-intersection problem allows arbitrary point sets and remains open. [Journal version, §5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-026-01958-z) · [Revised preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15332v2) · [Erdős, 1981, p. 35](https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1981-16.pdf).

## Priority search on 26 November 2026

- Compared both arXiv versions, the authors' copies and the journal text. Traced exact-title, DOI and arXiv-identifier citations, then searched combinations of *blocking pencil*, *union of plane curves*, *polynomial graph*, *vertical translates*, *permutation*, *missing lines*, *blocking semioval* and *logarithmic lower bound*. The journal DOI and its OpenAlex citing-works query each showed zero indexed citing records at the time of inspection. Semantic Scholar rate-limited its query. Newly published and unindexed work can escape these searches. [October 2025 author manuscript](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~dghioca/papers/blocking-union-Oct-13.pdf) · [September 2026 revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15332v2) · [Crossref record](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1007/s10623-026-01958-z) · [OpenAlex citing-works record](https://api.openalex.org/works?filter=cites:W4415879046).
- Ughi studied unions of conics. The conjecture authors credit Szőnyi's work on inversive planes and his observation about conics, and already give logarithmic order for arbitrary nonsingular conics in odd characteristic. Illés, Szőnyi and Wettl and Boros give examples with few different curves in small characteristic. The pencil result crosses these characteristics by requiring the same leading coefficient. The higher-degree result for arbitrary reflexive components uses the distinct hypothesis stated in its proof. [Ughi, 1988](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00183016) · [Asgarli–Ghioca–Yip, introduction and §§2–3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-026-01958-z).
- The same authors' earlier pencil work counts members that individually block all lines. Their separate family problem asks whether a union blocks. Related constructions of blocking semiovals concern particular unions with additional tangency requirements. Searches under these different formulations found no earlier general lower bound for the permutation regime. [Proportion of blocking curves in a pencil](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.06019) · [Blocking semiovals containing conics](https://doi.org/10.1515/advgeom-2012-0025).
- Entin identifies the geometric monodromy of reflexive curves and proves equidistribution at fixed degree. Cafure and Matera provide the explicit point bound used here when the number of curves grows with the field. The common-specialization argument in the pencil proof works also when reflexivity fails. The affine hyperplane minimum in the exact Frobenius calculation is the classical theorem of Jamison and of Brouwer and Schrijver. [Entin, Theorems 1–2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.05454) · [Cafure–Matera, Theorem 7.1](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0405302#S7) · [Brouwer–Schrijver](https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/9858/9858D.pdf).

## Reproduction

Run `python3 results/asgarli-ghioca-yip-blocking-pencil/verify.py` using Python 3.10 or later. It checks 266,184 shift sets over eight fields for every distinct field Frobenius power, directly against all nonvertical lines. Across two-, three- and four-degree examples it independently factors 367 regular seed instances, verifies 8,577 scaled graph–line incidences and checks the multiplicity bound on their parameters. It finds 145 simultaneous factor-type matches among these examples. Over the field of three elements it enumerates the two squarefree quartic types without rational roots, with respectively 72 and 24 rational root orderings. The written proofs establish the asymptotic assertions. [Verifier](verify.py) · [Pencil proof](PROOF.md) · [Frobenius case](FROBENIUS.md) · [Reflexive families](REFLEXIVE.md).

## Independent mathematical review

A second researcher checked the collision estimate, smooth rational specialization, geometric component descent, discriminant exclusions, explicit error terms, projective point at infinity and the line multiplicity bound in every characteristic. They separately verified the trace calculation and the surface argument under Entin’s hypotheses. Their independent order-32 calculation found all 62 affine binary hyperplanes for each of four nonidentity Frobenius powers. All 31,465 five-element shift sets containing zero missed a hyperplane, and zero with a basis met every hyperplane. They also reran the repository verifier.
