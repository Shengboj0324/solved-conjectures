# Sources and prior-resolution search

Jiang Zeng posted version 1 of *Two q-Deformations of Narayana Polynomials* on 20 October 2026 and version 2 on 13 November. Equation (1.3) defines the original kernel. Theorem 9.2 gives simple negative zeros. Theorem 9.3 gives $P_n(qt)\prec P_{n+1}(t)$, where the change of scale depends on which row is used. The first question in the closing section explicitly asks whether the *original* $P_n(t)$ and $P_{n+1}(t)$ strictly interlace for $0<q<1$ and $\theta>-1$. The separately numbered Conjecture 12.8 is about coefficientwise minors of four matrices. [Primary version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28691v2)

On 26 November I searched the original title and identifier, “unscaled interlacing”, the consecutive-row formulation, Rogers parameter shifts, terminating basic hypergeometric interlacing, generalized Stieltjes–Wigert polynomials, and logarithmic separation of $q$-polynomial zeros. OpenAlex returned zero citing records at that date. The Semantic Scholar API returned its rate-limit status, so it supplied no independent count. Both permanent repository branches were searched for the author, original identifier and normalized and unscaled statements. The earlier Fuss–Narayana research mentions Zeng's matrix theorem and addresses Wang–Yang's ordinary integer-parameter arrays. I found no previous proof of the unscaled all-parameter question in this bounded search. | Related result | Mathematical distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Lamprecht, real zeros and Suffridge convolution | This gives general theory of logarithmically $q$-separated roots, finite differences and multiplier sequences. The elementary sign tests in the proof belong to that established theory. The specific application uses the Pochhammer factors in Zeng's row and a reciprocal symmetry that changes with its degree. [Lamprecht](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.1102) |
| Stieltjes–Wigert and little $q$-Jacobi polynomials | The auxiliary $U_n$ is a reflected, rescaled Stieltjes–Wigert polynomial. Zeng's original row has additional factors depending on both $k$ and $n-k$. In little $q$-Jacobi notation its terminating series has top parameters $q^{-n},q^{-n-\theta}$ and lower parameter $q^{\theta+1}$. Making the second top parameter equal to $abq^{n+1}$ with $a=q^\theta$ forces $b=q^{-2n-2\theta-1}$ to vary with $n$. Ordinary fixed-parameter orthogonality consequently does not compare the two rows in the source's variable. [Stieltjes–Wigert formula](https://www.caop.org/qpolynomials/stieltjes_wigert) · [NIST DLMF, §18.27](https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.27) |
| Continuous and $q$-super Catalan Narayana families | Zeng's earlier continuous interpolation treats Gegenbauer moments and Hankel positivity. The recent $q$-super Catalan paper treats $\gamma$-positivity for two other Narayana polynomial families. Their assertions involve different polynomials or different positivity properties. [Continuous interpolation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19548) · [$q$-super Catalan paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15811) |
