# Named conjecture screening, 26 November 2026

Coordination is in [issue 156](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/156#issuecomment-7186443646). The following priority decisions concern the exact public statements.

| Candidate | Evidence and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Erdős’s interpolation minimum, proposed first correction (Erdős 1131) | The [problem](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1131) still prints the question whether the coefficient of $1/n$ is one. The [discussion](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/1131) links an [April 2026 disproof](https://www.overleaf.com/read/mhmzxwcsqjyd#c38015). Brutman and Toledano had earlier found numerical evidence against that coefficient. Exclude the coefficient-one claim from discovery work. Determining the optimal coefficient is a distinct question. |
| Erdős–Herzog–Piranian short-path question (Erdős 1041) | The [problem](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1041) asks for a path of length less than two connecting two roots. The [discussion](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/1041) links [ani’s September 2026 degree-seven counterexample](https://www.overleaf.com/read/ctmqrqwthkcn#bf8155) and William Cook’s [independent check](https://github.com/wcook04/plectis-erdos/tree/969dddcedcdd87907821e2c3db66a7fa8072fde8/lean/ErdosProblems/Erdos1041/Counterexample). Exclude. |
| Erdős–Herzog–Piranian extremal lemniscate length (Erdős 114) | A [November 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12830) claims the full length conjecture. Set aside on priority grounds. |
| Erdős–Straus distinct-distance subsets (Erdős 1088) | The [listed problem](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1088) asks about subexponential growth in dimension for each fixed target size. Howe, Huffman, Kohlmeyer and Kruer claim the general bound $2^{C_n\sqrt d\log_2(d+2)}$ in their [November manuscript](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00766v1). Treat the full assertion as claimed prior to this campaign. |
| Forest independence-sequence unimodality (Erdős 993) | Fang, Lu, Nevo, Yao and Zheng treat [sufficiently large forests](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20961). Li, Vallier and Zhang give a [second proof of the all-forests statement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07943) and attribute the first to Zhang and Li. Exclude the full assertion. |
| Erdős–Sárközy nonsquarefree products (Erdős 848) | The [discussion](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/848) records Sawhney’s result for sufficiently large $N$. Alex Chengyu Li’s [July manuscript](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7230480) claims the exact extremum $\lfloor(N+18)/25\rfloor$ for every positive $N$, including the diagonal condition. Exclude the all-$N$ statement on priority grounds. |
| Bollobás–Erdős–Tuza maximal-independent-set conjecture (Erdős 611) | The [original clique problem](https://doi.org/10.1016/0012-365X(92)90681-5) and [Cooper–Hollars, Conjecture 1.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15486) concern complementary graph formulations. An independent route through weighted transversals again gives a bound depending only on $c$ and modular width. The [20 November project proof](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/f66b4afd/docs/bollobas-erdos-tuza-research.md#a-constant-upper-bound-for-bounded-modular-width) already proves $\tau(G)\le c^{-(4k+1)}$ at modular width $k$. The same [issue](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/71) records the project’s $\Omega_c(\sqrt{n\log n})$ unrestricted lower bound. Exclude both bounds from new-result claims. The unrestricted sublinear upper bound remains the question. |
| Erdős’s exterior power-sum question (Erdős 973) | Luo, Yang and Zhu disprove exponential decay of the optimal maximum. Tan, Wang, Huang and Chen independently give a quantitative improvement and credit the first resolution. [Original question](https://www.erdosproblems.com/973) · [Luo–Yang–Zhu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22017) · [Tan–Wang–Huang–Chen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02043) |
| Erdős–Herzog–Piranian lemniscate area (Erdős 1040) | The problem record reports distinct area infima for two sets of capacity zero. For the separate vanishing question, Tzachristas proves the missing capacity-one theorem and deduces vanishing for all closed infinite sets of transfinite diameter at least one. Both questions have prior answers. [Problem and counterexample reference](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1040) · [Tzachristas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06050) |
| Erdős’s prescribed interpolation cluster sets (Erdős 1151) | Chojecki and Hart already give the fixed-point classification in Theorem 1.1. Every nonempty closed target occurs at every fixed point. The empty target occurs exactly at the cosine points with rational angle divided by π of odd denominator. Section 7 treats other readings of the wording. [Author manuscript](https://www.ulam.ai/research/erdos1151.pdf) · [Problem](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1151) |
| Erdős’s interpolation arrays (Erdős 671) | Liam Price’s June announcement gives an array with unbounded Lebesgue functions at every point for which every continuous function has a point of convergence. This stronger answer addresses both questions in the original discussion. [Price’s announcement](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/671) · [Problem](https://www.erdosproblems.com/671) |
| Erdős’s cube-tiling cutoff (Erdős 769) | The general lower bound $c(n)\gg n^n$ already has an August disproof along odd dimensions. The geometric statement and the prime-successor refinement have different quantifiers. Exclude the general lower bound. [Burgess–Erdős](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1974-27.pdf) · [Prior claim and source record](https://probxiv.com/problems/erdos-769) |
| Erdős’s lacunary bounded-gap sumset question (Erdős 1112) | Johan Land’s manuscript, with a simplification credited to Stijn Cambie, gives both threshold directions: a fixed ratio works exactly when $d_2\ge k+1$. Exclude the threshold problem. [Author’s paper and statement](https://github.com/beetree/math_erdos_1112) · [Discussion](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/1112) |
| Erdős–Szabó arithmetic-progression intersections (Erdős 272) | Zhanfu Yang gives the exact maximum over families with a common element for every $N$ in the stated range. Whether the unrestricted optimum can always be attained in this way remains separate. Exclude the starred-family maximum. [Yang, Theorem 1.4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23004) · [Problem record](https://www.erdosproblems.com/272) |
| Erdős–Sárközy–Sós small maximal Sidon sets (Erdős 156) | Howe, Huffman, Kohlmeyer and Kruer give an inclusion-maximal Sidon set of size $O(N^{1/3})$ in every sufficiently large integer interval, the requested order. Their paper is dated November 2, 2026. Exclude the existence claim. [Primary manuscript, abstract and Theorem 1](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00767v1) · [Original problem](https://www.erdosproblems.com/156) |
| Erdős’s prime divisors of partition numbers (Erdős 1106) | Schinzel's argument already proves divergence of the number of different primes dividing the first $n$ partition numbers. Ono supplies a second route through partition congruences. The stronger eventual comparison with $n$ remains a separate question. [Schinzel's lemma in Erdős–Ivić](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1987-32b.pdf) · [Ono](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2000/151-1/p09) · [Original problem](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1106) |
| Erdős’s Mersenne divisor-sum ratios (Erdős 893) | Kovač and Luca prove the doubling ratios have infinite limsup, excluding every finite limit. Their paper leaves eventual divergence to infinity open. [Kovač–Luca](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04883) · [Original problem](https://www.erdosproblems.com/893) |
