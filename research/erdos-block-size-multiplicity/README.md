# Erdős–Purdy conjecture

[Tracking issue 780](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/780). Research started 26 November 2026.

A pairwise balanced design on $n$ points has proper blocks of size at least two, and every unordered pair of points lies in exactly one block. Write $m_t$ for the number of blocks of size $t$ and $f(n)$ for the minimum of $\max_t m_t$ over these designs. Erdős and Purdy asked whether $f(n)=O(\sqrt n)$ for every sufficiently large $n$. The constant must be independent of $n$ and $t$. In 1978 they also conjectured the stronger asymptotic $f(n)=c\sqrt n+o(\sqrt n)$ in equation (5). The order bound here is the separately stated 1981 problem. Gyárfás names it Conjecture 1 in §2. Erdős repeated it in 1981 and with Duke, Fowler and Phelps in 1985. [Erdős and Purdy (1978), pp. 207–208](https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1978-42.pdf) · [Gyárfás (2002), p. 369](https://users.renyi.hu/~gyarfas/Cikkek/101_Gyarfas_ErdosProblemsOnIrregularitiesOfLineSizesAndPointDegrees.pdf) · [Erdős (1981)](https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1981-16.pdf) · [Erdős, Duke, Fowler and Phelps (1985)](https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1985-12.pdf) · [Bloom, problem 734](https://www.erdosproblems.com/734)

## Prior work and scope

- De Bruijn and Erdős proved that the number of blocks is at least $n$. Pair counting gives $\sum_t t(t-1)m_t=n(n-1)$. Thus at most $n/4$ blocks have size greater than $\lceil 2\sqrt n\rceil$, and some size at most that cutoff occurs $\Omega(\sqrt n)$ times. The 1985 paper also asks about a bound $m_t\le C\sqrt t$. The very same popular size has $m_t/\sqrt t=\Omega(n^{1/4})$, ruling out that stronger proposal. The main $\sqrt n$ question survives. A concurrent researcher also recorded the distinction in the [coordination thread](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/156). [De Bruijn and Erdős (1948)](https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1948-01.pdf)
- Colbourn, Phelps and Rödl counted possible multisets and sets of block sizes. Erdős, Duke, Fowler and Phelps studied the maximum number of distinct block sizes. Gyárfás corrected the exact value in Theorem 1. These questions allow a single size to recur arbitrarily often. Erdős and Larson instead optimized the minimum block size. [Colbourn, Phelps and Rödl (1984)](https://doi.org/10.4153/CMB-1984-057-0) · [Erdős and Larson](https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1982-13.pdf) · [Erdős, Duke, Fowler and Phelps](https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1985-12.pdf)
- Erdős and Purdy proved $f(n)=O(n^{3/4})$ by random restriction, with the probability estimates omitted. Gyárfás credits Blokhuis, Szőnyi and Wilbrink with the concentration obstruction for this construction. [Erdős and Purdy, Theorem 2](https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1978-42.pdf) · [Gyárfás, §2](https://users.renyi.hu/~gyarfas/Cikkek/101_Gyarfas_ErdosProblemsOnIrregularitiesOfLineSizesAndPointDegrees.pdf)
- Zribi constructs a design from a conjectural shifted-correlation code over a prime field. The April note requires a flat support-size distribution and exact coverage of pair differences. The July working report also identifies an order-selection gap in the stated implication and gives the dense projective-plane restriction obstruction. Both are credited as prior work. [Zribi (30 April 2026)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dp5tbTjJR3t1jdvDUO90U3gxZp2nxySe/view) · [July working report](https://erdosproblemaday.com/report/734) · [Problem discussion](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/734)
- Bado obtained a fractional solution for admissible profiles and explicit characteristic-two difference packings. His three trace families cover some differences three times, and the packing with flat size counts leaves uncovered differences. Neither supplies an exact cover with the required multiplicities. [Bado (September 2026), §§7–10](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/412305325_Difference_families_and_pairwise_balanced_designs)
- The 26 November search covered the original papers, the discussion and proof claims, the community database, finite-linear-space and pairwise-balanced-design literature under block sizes, line multiplicities and frequency, and both repository branches, issues and review PRs. No complete construction or impossibility theorem for the stated bound was located. The community database recorded `open` with three forum posts. [Dated database](https://github.com/teorth/erdosproblems/blob/3d9fd1caec27bb400ff4a92a513a29965bd99695/data/problems.yaml) · [Research wiki](https://erdosproblems.ai/wiki/research/design_size_multiplicity)

The research question is whether general designs can spread their block sizes across a range of order $\sqrt n$ without leaving a size with excessive multiplicity. The [restriction theorem](restriction.md) reduces the all-orders conjecture to any sequence of orders with bounded consecutive ratios. It also repairs the prime-selection step in Zribi’s conditional construction. Constructing the required designs or codes remains open.

[Point symmetry](symmetry.md) prevents any point-transitive design itself from meeting the proposed bound. At prime-power orders such a design has a size occurring at least a constant times $v^{3/4}$.

The [window criterion](windows.md) shows that the conjecture is equivalent to constructing designs with average multiplicity $O(\sqrt v)$ on consecutive square-to-square intervals of sizes. Around $\sqrt v$, a single size can then occur $O(v^{3/4})$ times, provided the entire interval meets the same total bound. An exact-order restriction smooths all the intervals simultaneously.
