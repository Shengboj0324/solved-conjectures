# Sources and priority audit

The authors posed this question explicitly. Independent review on 26 November 2026 identified an earlier theorem whose elementary blow-up answers it for unrestricted graphs. The tree and fixed-rank quantitative arguments were separately checked. [Review receipt](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/782#issuecomment-7187707661)

## Why the unrestricted answer was already available

Fix one earlier tree $T$ and a rank $r$ at which every maximizing vertex is a nonleaf. Replace each vertex by a clique of order $t$, and put every edge between the cliques of adjacent vertices. An independent $r$-set uses at most one clone from any clique and projects to an independent $r$-set of $T$. Each clone of $v$ belongs to exactly $t^{r-1}s_r(v)$ such sets. Its degree is $t(1+\deg_T v)-1$. The minimum degree and the degree of each maximizing center are consequently separated by at least $t$ on $t|V(T)|$ vertices. This proves a linear gap from the earlier tree by a standard operation. The operation produces cliques, so the separate tree construction addresses an additional restriction. The fixed-rank construction in this note also attains the sharper gap $n-o(n)$ at every $r\ge3$. [Feghali–Johnson–Thomas](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.07741) · [Independent review](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/782#issuecomment-7187707661)
